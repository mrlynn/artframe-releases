## Monet. Hokusai. Deep space. Your vacation photos.

ArtFrame replaces your TV's idle screensaver with art worth leaving on. **ArtFrameRemote**, the Mac app, handles setup, finds the art, and sends it to your TV. The TV app is included.

- **A world of art in 4K.** Discover open-access paintings from the Art Institute of Chicago and Cleveland Museum of Art, photography from Wikimedia Commons, NASA and Unsplash, or add your own pictures.
- **Paintings stay whole.** No cropping a masterpiece to fit a widescreen. Art sits on a soft, blurred wash of its own colors, with a museum-style label for the title, artist, year and collection.
- **Pick a mood.** Thirteen themes span Impressionism, Japanese prints, deep space, oceans, forests and the seasons. Or search for a painter or subject.
- **A little life between paintings.** Optional clock, weather, calendar, markets and news cards put your day a glance away. Prefer just the art? Keep it that way.
- **Built with OLEDs in mind.** Gentle drift, cross-fades and configurable night dimming help reduce static content. They don't guarantee protection from burn-in.
- **Sony BRAVIA extras.** Adjust picture, sound and power from your Mac, including turning the picture off while music keeps playing.

**No account required.** Your art is stored on your Mac and copied to your TV over your home network. Once installed, ArtFrame runs on the TV on its own; your Mac only needs to be on when you change art or settings.

## From idle screen to gallery in three steps

1. **[Download ArtFrameRemote for Mac](https://github.com/mrlynn/artframe-releases/releases/latest)** and open the disk image.
2. **Connect your TV.** The setup assistant walks you through enabling Developer options and debugging, approving your Mac on the TV, and installing the included screensaver.
3. **Choose your art and send it over.** Pick a theme, search, or add your own photos. The next time your TV goes idle, it's a gallery.

Start with the **[setup guide](https://artframe.mlynn.dev/setup)** and **[guide for your TV or streaming device](https://artframe.mlynn.dev/tvs)**.

### Will it work on my TV?

You'll need a **Mac running macOS 14 or later** for setup and a **Google TV or Android TV** device on your home network. Sony BRAVIA is tested on hardware; other brands and streaming devices have model-specific caveats covered in the TV guides.

Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Roku, Fire TV, VIDAA and Apple TV can't run ArtFrame directly. A compatible Google TV or Android TV streaming device connected by HDMI lets you use ArtFrame with those screens, too.
